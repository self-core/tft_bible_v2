# Set 16 (Lore & Legends) - Accurate Data

## Set Overview
- **Name**: Set 16: Lore & Legends
- **Release Date**: December 3rd, 2025
- **Theme**: Dive into Runeterra's history with a massive Library of Lore
- **Core Mechanic**: "Unlockables" - Complete objectives to unlock champions
- **Total Champions**: 100 (60 base champions + 40 unlockable champions)
- **Special Features**: 7-cost champions, combined champions, Team-Up Augments

## Core Set Mechanics
### Unlockables Mechanic
- Complete small objectives during gameplay to unlock powerful new champions
- Once unlocked, champions are added to the pool and guaranteed to appear in your first shop refresh
- 40 champions can be unlocked in total
- Special rewards for finishing in top 4 with each unlocked champion

### Combined Champions
- Unique units that bring together two kits in a single body (e.g., Kobuko & Yuumi, Lucian & Senna)

### 7-Cost Champions
- Rare, powerful units that feel like "legends you've earned"
- Guaranteed to appear in your very next shop refresh after being unlocked
- Completely reshape a fight when fielded
