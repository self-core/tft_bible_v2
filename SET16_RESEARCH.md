# Set 16 (Lore & Legends) Research Data - Updated

## Set Information
- **Name**: Set 16: Lore & Legends
- **Release Date**: December 3rd, 2025
- **Theme**: Dive into Runeterra's history with a massive Library of Lore
- **Core Mechanic**: "Unlockables" - Complete objectives to unlock champions
- **Total Champions**: 100 (60 base champions + 40 unlockable champions)
- **Special Features**: 7-cost champions, combined champions, Team-Up Augments

## Core Set Mechanics
### Unlockables Mechanic
- Complete small objectives during gameplay to unlock powerful new champions
- Once unlocked, champions are added to the pool and guaranteed to appear in your first shop refresh
- 40 champions can be unlocked in total
- Special rewards for finishing in top 4 with each unlocked champion

### Region-Based Traits
- Each region of Runeterra forms its own trait
- Regions include: Demacia, Noxus, Bilgewater, Piltover, Zaun, Ionia, Shadow Isles, Freljord, Shurima, Void, Targon, Ixtal
- Each region has unique mechanics and bonuses

## Traits for Set 16

### Region Traits

#### Demacia
- **Effect**: Each time your team loses 25% max Health, Demacians RALLY, reducing the cost of their abilities by 10%. Demacians gain Armor and Magic Resist.
- **Breakpoints**:
  - 3 units: 12 Armor & MR
  - 5 units: 25 Armor & MR
  - 7 units: 25 Armor & MR. On Rally, smite enemies for 5% of their max Health.
  - 11 units: 150 Armor & MR. BATTLE FOR DEMACIA!

#### Noxus
- **Effect**: After the enemy team has lost 15% of their Health, summon Atakhan, Bringer of Ruin. Each Noxian champion's star level increases his power.
- **Breakpoints**:
  - 3 units: He slashes enemies, dealing magic damage.
  - 5 units: Noxians get more powerful the longer he is alive.
  - 7 units: He drains the souls of enemies on cast.
  - 10 units: After 10 seconds, BRING RUIN.

#### Void
- **Effect**: Gain Mutations that only Void champions can use. Void champions gain Attack Speed.
- **Breakpoints**:
  - 2 units: 1 Mutation, 8% AS
  - 4 units: 2 Mutations, 18% AS
  - 6 units: 3 Mutations, 28% AS
  - 9 units: Mutations become supercharged, increasing their power by 50%. 35% AS

#### Bilgewater
- **Effect**: Each round, gain Silver Serpents (Serpents), plus 2 additional for each Bilgewater takedown. Silver Serpents can be spent in the Black Market to grant Bilgewater champions bonus stats and special loot. Higher tiers unlock rarer loot.
- **Breakpoints**:
  - 3 units: 18 Serpents
  - 5 units: 30 Serpents, improved loot
  - 7 units: 55 Serpents, superior loot
  - 10 units: 150 Serpents, 2x stats, FIRE CANNONS!

#### Ionia
- **Effect**: Ionians follow a Path that grants them different bonuses (Spirit, Generosity, Enlightenment, Transcendence, Precision). Effects include bonus HP, stacking AD/AP on casts, gold from takedowns, XP gains, critical strike mechanics, and more depending on path.
- **Breakpoints**:
  - 3 units: Path bonuses begin to apply (Spirit/Generosity style bonuses)
  - 5 units: Additional path bonuses, increased stacking and gold rewards
  - 7 units: Major path bonuses and powerful team effects
  - 10 units: Grand path: ultimate bonuses and powerful awakenings

#### Shadow Isles
- **Effect**: Whenever ANY champion dies, gain Souls. Shadow Isles champions' abilities are empowered based on Souls collected.
- **Breakpoints**:
  - 2 units: 1x Souls, 18% AD & AP
  - 3 units: 1.30x Souls, 20% AD & AP
  - 4 units: 1.60x Souls, 22% AD & AP
  - 5 units: 1.90x Souls, 25% AD & AP

#### Ixtal
- **Effect**: Complete Quests to earn Sunshards and spend them for loot! Completing a Quest grants you a new one at the start of next round.
- **Breakpoints**:
  - 3 units: Complete Quests to earn Sunshards and spend them for loot!
  - 5 units: Heal 2 player Health at the start of each player combat
  - 7 units: After winning combat, find 50 Ixtal and unearth bonus loot

### Story Traits

#### Cultist
- **Effect**: Cultists gain attack speed and damage during combat. The effect increases as combat continues.
- **Breakpoints**:
  - 2 units: Cultists gain +20% attack speed and +10% damage
  - 4 units: Cultists gain +45% attack speed and +20% damage
  - 6 units: Cultists gain +80% attack speed and +35% damage

#### Ascendant
- **Effect**: After each combat, an Ascendant Charm appears in your shop. These are powerful effects that can be bought for gold.
- **Breakpoints**:
  - 1 unit: After each combat, an Ascendant Charm appears in your shop

#### Assimilator (Kai'Sa)
- **Effect**: Kai'Sa has a different Ability depending if her Attack Damage or Ability Power is higher.
- **Breakpoints**:
  - 1 unit: Kai'Sa's ability changes based on whether AD or AP is higher

## Champion Classes/Traits

### Universal Traits (Non-region-specific)
#### Arcanist
- Your team gains Ability Power, Arcanists gain more.
- **Breakpoints**: 2/4/6 units

#### Bruiser
- Your team gains 150 maximum Health. Bruisers gain more.
- **Breakpoints**: 2/4/6 units

#### Defender
- Your team gains 12 Armor and Magic Resist. Defenders gain more.
- **Breakpoints**: 2/4/6 units

#### Disruptor
- Disruptors' abilities Dazzle enemies hit. Disruptors deal bonus damage to Dazzled enemies.
- **Breakpoints**: 2/4 units

#### Gunslinger
- Gunslingers gain Attack Damage. Every 4th attack from Gunslingers deals bonus physical damage.
- **Breakpoints**: 2/4 units

#### Invoker
- Your team gains 1 Mana Regen. Invokers gain more Mana from all sources.
- **Breakpoints**: 2/4 units

#### Juggernaut
- Juggernauts gain Durability, increased while above 50% health. When a Juggernaut dies, other Juggernauts heal for a portion of their max Health.
- **Breakpoints**: 2/4/6 units

#### Longshot
- Longshots gain Damage Amp and deal more damage to targets farther away.
- **Breakpoints**: 2/3/4/5 units

#### Quickstriker
- Your team gains Attack Speed. Quickstrikers gain bonus Attack Speed based on their target's missing Health.
- **Breakpoints**: 2/3/4/5 units

#### Slayer
- Slayers gain Omnivamp and Attack Damage. They increase the bonus Attack Damage by up to 50% based on their missing Health.
- **Breakpoints**: 2/4/6 units

#### Vanquisher
- Vanquishers' abilities can critically strike. They also gain Critical Strike Chance and Critical Strike Damage.
- **Breakpoints**: 2/3/4/5 units

#### Warden
- When Wardens first drop below 75% and 25% Health, they gain a Shield based on their max Health.
- **Breakpoints**: 2/3/4/5 units

## Notable Unlockable Champions

### Kai'Sa (Void, Marksman, Assimilator)
- **Cost**: 3
- **Ability**: "Killer Instinct" - Her ability changes based on whether Attack Damage or Ability Power is higher
- **Mechanic**: Adapts to your build

### Sylas (Chainbreaker)
- **Cost**: 3
- **Ability**: Rotates between 3 different Abilities, depending on which one is most useful at the moment
- **Mechanic**: Cannot cast the same ability twice in a row

### Azir (Emperor)
- **Cost**: 4
- **Ability**: Deploys two Guards who can be placed anywhere on the battlefield
- **Mechanic**: Guards do not move or attack, and die when Azir does

### Aatrox (World Ender)
- **Cost**: 5
- **Ability**: Gains Attack Damage equal to his Omnivamp; on first death, briefly becomes untargetable and heals back to full Health
